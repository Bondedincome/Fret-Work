    How to set it up
    1. Prop your phone or laptop so the camera sees the neck from the headstock to about the 7th fret, as large as possible.
    2. Press Calibrate neck. The picture freezes: press and slide to aim with the zoom loupe, then let go on each of the 4 points.
    3. If a line is slightly off, use Fine-tune corners and drag the handles.
    4. With Follow guitar movement on, the guides stick to the neck as you move. The badge in the corner shows if tracking is locked.
    5. Ghost dots show where each finger goes. Green = correct. Hold the chord for a moment to score it.
    6. Use bright, even light, and avoid a window behind you. Plain clothing behind the neck helps tracking.
